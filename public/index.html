Transparent by design · Responsible AI use

AI-assisted research. Human-reviewed decisions.

AI + HUMAN OVERSIGHT

Generative AI may be used to speed up research organization, segmentation, first drafts, data cleaning and reporting preparation. AI is a support tool, not a substitute for verification, commercial judgment or client approval.

Where AI can assist

  • Organizing public company information and research notes
  • Suggesting lead segments and qualification questions
  • Drafting outreach options and follow-up language
  • Structuring spreadsheet, pipeline and report templates

What is checked by a person

  • Prospect fit, contact relevance and available sources
  • Research accuracy and removal of unsupported claims
  • Message tone, personalization and business context
  • Final deliverables, priorities and next-step recommendations
Our commitment: No invented leads or performance claims. No autonomous prospect outreach. Client-confidential information is not entered into external AI tools without prior permission. Any automations or integrations are discussed and approved before use.

Client information · Working safeguards

Your commercial information is treated as confidential.

Once we begin working together, we may need access to prospect lists, sales pipelines, proposals or internal processes. We agree on the information, tools and access needed before work starts. These safeguards apply to the engagement itself, not just the initial contact form.

01 / Minimum necessary access

Only information relevant to the agreed scope is requested. Where practical, we work inside your approved tools, with access limited to the tasks you authorize.

02 / Confidentiality & separation

Your lists, strategies, client details and sales records are not sold, published, reused for another client or displayed as portfolio work without explicit written approval.

03 / Clear AI boundaries

AI may support work with public information or non-sensitive templates. Confidential materials, private CRM exports or non-public client data are not submitted to external AI systems without your prior, specific approval.

04 / Agreed handover

Access permissions, any external tools, how work files are shared, and data return or deletion at the end of the engagement are agreed with you. Confidentiality terms can be documented before access is provided.

Before any access is granted, we confirm the approved workspace, any third-party tools and the confidentiality terms appropriate to your project.

Practical questions, answered

Before we start.

What is included in the business development partnership?

We agree on a scope combining commercial discovery, lead qualification, outreach preparation, CRM/pipeline organization and progress reporting. We do not assume all activities are needed on day one; priorities are selected together and documented before starting.

Can I hire PIPELINEER by the hour instead?

Yes. Hourly support can cover focused tasks such as Excel research, CRM cleanup, outreach drafts or reporting. We agree on a time budget, tasks, rate and review process in advance.

When will I see the first deliverables?

The first agreed milestone and delivery date are set during kickoff, based on scope, data availability and weekly hours. For a partnership, the initial phase normally focuses on discovery, qualification criteria and a small pilot before scaling the workflow.

Will you contact prospects on my behalf?

Only if it is included in the agreed scope. Target lists, channels, message approach and account access are reviewed first. Research and writing support can also be delivered without sending any outreach.

How do you use artificial intelligence?

AI tools may support organizing research, developing drafts and formatting reports. Information and client-facing materials are manually checked. We will not put confidential client information into third-party AI tools or activate automated outreach without your permission.

What if I already use a CRM, or prefer spreadsheets?

We work with an appropriate existing setup where feasible, including spreadsheets or lightweight CRM workflows. There is no requirement to buy a complex platform to begin. New subscriptions or integrations are proposed before any cost is incurred.

Do you guarantee meetings, sales or revenue?

No. We commit to agreed research quality, process work and transparent reporting; sales outcomes depend on your offer, timing, market and buyer response. Any measurable targets are agreed as working goals, not guarantees.

How is my CRM and confidential business information handled after we start working together?

Before accessing internal information, we agree on the scope, approved tools and minimum necessary permissions. Your data is not resold, shared across clients or used in public examples without written authorization. Confidential information is not submitted to external AI tools without specific prior approval. We also agree on handover, retention or deletion at the end of the engagement. See our working safeguards.
